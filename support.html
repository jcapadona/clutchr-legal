Subscriptions & Billing

Clutchr Pro subscriptions are managed entirely through your Apple ID. To cancel, change plans, or request a refund, go to:

Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad.

We don't have access to your payment details — all billing is handled by Apple.
