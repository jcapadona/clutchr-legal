CHILDREN'S PRIVACY NOTICE

Effective Date: June 30, 2026
Last Updated: June 30, 2026

Clutchr Baseball ("Clutchr," "we," "us," or "our") is used by baseball athletes of many ages, including children under 13. This notice explains, in plain terms, how we handle information from young athletes and what parents and guardians need to know. It supplements our full Privacy Policy and should be read alongside it.

1. Who This Applies To

This notice applies to any user of Clutchr Baseball who is under the age of 13 at the time they create an account, and to the parents and legal guardians of those users.

2. Our Approach to Children's Privacy

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children under 13 than is reasonably necessary to operate the app, and we do not use children's information for behavioral advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. What Information We Collect From Children

If a child under 13 uses Clutchr Baseball, we may collect:

  • Account information: An email address and display name, provided through Google sign-in (via our authentication provider, Supabase). We do not receive the child's Google password.
  • Athletic profile information: Position/role (Pitcher, Catcher, Hitter, Infielder, Outfielder), age band, and season phase, selected during onboarding.
  • In-app activity: Lesson progress, completed reps, streaks, saved cues, and similar content the child creates or generates while using the app.
  • Approximate location for weather features: With device permission, general location is used to display local weather relevant to practice/game planning. This is not used for tracking or advertising.

We do not knowingly collect a child's precise GPS coordinates beyond what is needed for the weather feature, photos, video, audio recordings, or contact lists from children.

4. Parental Consent

Before a child under 13 creates an account, a parent or legal guardian should set up the account or directly supervise account creation, and should review this notice and our full Privacy Policy.

By allowing a child to use Clutchr Baseball, a parent or guardian consents to the collection and use of the child's information as described in this notice. Parents may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us (see Section 7).

5. How We Use Children's Information

We use information collected from children solely to:

  • Operate their account and save their training progress
  • Route them to age-appropriate, role-specific lesson content
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the app
  • Communicate with the account holder about app updates, if notifications are enabled

We do not use children's information to serve targeted advertising. Clutchr Baseball does not run third-party ads.

6. Sharing Children's Information

We do not sell children's personal information. We share it only with the service providers necessary to operate the app — currently Supabase (data storage/authentication) and, for subscription accounts, Apple and RevenueCat (subscription billing). We do not share children's information with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Parental Rights

As a parent or legal guardian, you have the right to:

  • Review the personal information we have collected from your child
  • Request deletion of your child's account and associated data
  • Refuse to permit further collection or use of your child's information, and request that we stop collecting it
  • Ask questions about our data practices for children

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@clutchrbaseball.com. We will respond and process deletion requests within 30 days. We may ask for reasonable information to verify you are the child's parent or guardian before fulfilling a request.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain a child's information only as long as the account remains active. Upon a verified parental deletion request, we will delete the child's account information from our active systems within 30 days, except where retention is required to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

9. Changes to This Notice

If we make material changes to how we handle children's information, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify account holders directly.
